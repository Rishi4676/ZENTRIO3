Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Our Commitment: We aim for 100% client satisfaction. If you have concerns about a payment or service, please contact us within 7 days. We review every request fairly and promptly.

1. General Refund Philosophy

Zentrio AI provides custom software development and consulting services. As our work is bespoke and time/resource-intensive, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on the stage of work completed at the time of the request.

2. Refund Eligibility Table

Scenario Refund Status Timeline
Duplicate payment charged accidentally Full Refund 5–7 business days
Service not started within 7 days of payment Full Refund 5–7 business days
Request raised within 48 hours of payment, work not started Full Refund 5–7 business days
Work partially completed (less than 30%) Partial Refund (up to 70%) 7–10 business days

Work more than 70% complete No Refund N/A

Cancellation requested after 7 days of payment No Refund N/A
Payment gateway error / technical failure Full Refund 3–5 business days

3. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund request:

  • Email us at zentriotechnology3@gmail.com within 7 days of payment.
  • Use the subject line: "Refund Request – [Your Invoice Number]"
  • Include: your registered email, payment ID (from your invoice), reason for refund, and any supporting details.

We will acknowledge your request within 1 business day and provide a decision within 3–5 business days.

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay back to your original payment method:

  • UPI / Net Banking: 3–5 business days
  • Credit/Debit Card: 5–7 business days (may vary by bank)
  • Wallets: 1–3 business days

Refund amounts will be credited to the same account/card used for the original payment. We cannot process refunds to a different account.

5. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel an ongoing project engagement at any time. Upon cancellation:

  • Work completed up to the cancellation date will be delivered to you.
  • Payment for completed milestones is non-refundable.
  • Any advance payment for future milestones will be refunded per the table in Section 2.

6. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund under any circumstances:

  • Subscription fees for completed billing periods.
  • Payments for fully delivered and client-accepted projects.
  • Third-party service costs incurred on your behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, API licenses).
  • Payments made more than 30 days ago.

7. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our refund decision, you may escalate the dispute through Razorpay's dispute resolution mechanism or contact your bank/card issuer for a chargeback. We will cooperate fully with any legitimate dispute process. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account termination.
