## Editor & Production Instructions:
1. **Color Profile:** Keep a high-contrast dark aesthetic, focusing heavily on **Cyan (#00dfd8)** and **Indigo (#6366f1)** neon lights.
2. **Text Transitions:** Use sharp, digital glitch or bounce transitions for the text overlays matching the speed of the music beats.
3. **Pacing:** Keep frame cuts under 1 second during the beat drop (seconds 5 to 10) to generate high viewer retention.
