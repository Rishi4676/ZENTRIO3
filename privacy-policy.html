Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Summary: Zentrio AI collects only what's necessary to provide our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. All payment transactions are processed securely via Razorpay.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, phone number when you register.
  • Payment Information: Payment method details are collected and processed by Razorpay. We store only payment status, amount, and transaction IDs — never raw card data.
  • Project Data: Project descriptions, uploaded files, and communication related to services you request.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, IP address, browser type, and device information for analytics and security.
  • Communication Data: Emails and messages you send through our contact forms or support channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our AI services.
  • Processing payments and sending transaction confirmations and invoices.
  • Sending service updates, project status notifications, and support responses.
  • Ensuring platform security and preventing fraud.
  • Complying with legal obligations under Indian law.

3. Payment Processing

All payments on Zentrio AI are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateway. When you make a payment:

  • Your payment details are transmitted directly to Razorpay's secure servers.
  • We receive only a payment ID, order ID, and transaction status.
  • We never store your card number, CVV, or UPI PIN on our servers.
  • All transactions are protected with 256-bit TLS encryption.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing.
  • Google Firebase — for authentication and database services.
  • Service providers — who assist in operating our platform under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal authorities — when required by applicable Indian law or valid legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us at zentriotechnology3@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (session tokens) and security (CSRF protection). We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser, but this may affect your ability to log in.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption, bcrypt password hashing, JWT-based authentication, CSRF protection, and rate limiting. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Under Indian IT laws and global best practices, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zentriotechnology3@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
